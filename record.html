🎙️ Coach Recording Booth

Record the seven Coach's Clinic lines in your own voice. Tap Record, read the line with match-day energy, tap Stop, listen back, re-record as often as you like, then Download each clip.

Please open this page in Safari. This browser records a format iPads can't play. Safari records .m4a, which works everywhere Hoop Maths runs.
Coach's tips: quiet room · hold the device about 30 cm away · smile while you read (it changes the sound) · say the numbers clearly · leave half a second of silence at the start and end. "SLAM!" deserves your full commentator voice.
When you're done: you'll have coach-0.m4a … coach-6.m4a in your Downloads. Move them into the repo's audio/ folder (~/ScupperLab/hoop-maths/audio/) and tell Claude — the app will use your voice from then on, with the robot voice only as a fallback.
